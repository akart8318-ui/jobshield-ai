Problem Statement

The Epidemic of Employment Scams

Over 64% of college students receive fraudulent job offers or task scams each semester.

1. The Upfront Fee Trap

Students are extorted into paying "Registration Fees", "Laptop Security Deposits", or "Verification Kit Charges" (₹500 - ₹5,000) for fake job postings.

2. Telegram / WhatsApp Task Scams

"Earn ₹3,000/day for liking YouTube videos". Small payouts hook victims before locking their savings into fraudulent investment portals.

3. The Local Vacancy Blindspot

Street flyers, posters on coaching walls, and local listings lack any verifiable digital footprint or corporate registration.

Our Solution

JobShield AI: 4 Pillars of Defense

An end-to-end multi-modal verification platform that analyzes offers before students commit time or money.

01

Omni-Input Vision

Upload street posters, WhatsApp screenshots, or PDF offer letters. Gemini Vision + OCR extracts entities without manual typing.

02

Heuristic Audit

Scans for extortion red flags: security deposits, interview kit charges, urgency triggers, and impossible pay-to-work ratios.

03

Live OSINT Checks

Inspects live DNS with WHOIS to catch newly minted scam domains (< 30 days old) and verifies physical shop/office addresses on Google Places.

04

Trust Score & Report

Generates a Trust Index (0-100%) with itemized proof points and a 1-click Cyber Crime reporting template (Portal 1930).
